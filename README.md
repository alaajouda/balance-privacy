Privacy Policy for Balance
Last Updated: November 2026
Balance is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application (Balance).
1. Information We Collect
Account Information: When you create an account or sign in (via Firebase Authentication), we collect your name and email address to manage your profile and recipes.
User-Generated Content: We collect and store the recipes, ingredients, estimated costs, instructions, and meal images you upload and share within the app (stored securely via Cloud Firestore and Supabase Storage).
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
Create, authenticate, and manage your user account.
Display recipes you publish to other users and allow you to edit or delete your own content.
Improve app functionality, user experience, and overall performance.
3. Data Storage, Security & Third-Party Services
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
Your data is stored and processed using trusted, industry-standard cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase and Supabase) with strict access controls.
4. Your Rights & Account Deletion
Content Management: You may edit or delete any recipes you have posted at any time directly within the app.
Account Deletion: You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time by contacting us via the email address provided below.
5. Children's Privacy
Our application is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
